Iron Wire

Song-dynasty Ge ware was prized for its cracks: a bold dark net called iron wire with a fine golden net, golden thread, running between. Fire a tile, let it cool, and watch a glaze tear itself into cells by one rule: every new crack meets an old one at a right angle.

Why a glaze cracks

A glaze is a thin coat of glass fused to clay. In the kiln both are hot and relaxed. As they cool, each shrinks, and if the glass shrinks more than the clay underneath it, the glass ends up stretched like a drumskin. Glass is strong when squeezed and weak when pulled, so a stretched glaze eventually lets go. Potters call it crazing. On most pots it is a fault. Ge ware made it the point.

The right-angle rule

Once a crack has run, the glaze beside it can no longer be pulled along the crack's length, only across it. So the next crack to start nearby runs straight away from the first and stops dead when it reaches another crack, making a T. That is the only rule this page follows. Every crack ends on another crack or on the edge, which is why the cells close up.

Two nets

金丝铁线, jīnsī tiěxiàn, golden thread and iron wire. The first, bolder cracks on Ge ware read black, and a finer network between them reads honey-gold. How the two colours got there is still argued over: rubbed-in stains, iron from the clay, tea and years of handling. Here you stain the first net by hand, then let the second form slowly and take its colour from use.

Words

Crazing
A net of fine cracks in a glaze that runs through the glass but not into the clay.
Glaze fit
How well a glaze's shrinkage matches its clay. A good fit leaves the glass slightly squeezed and it never crazes. The fit buttons above set how far off it is.
Delayed crazing
Cracks that appear months or years after firing, as the clay slowly takes up moisture and swells, stretching the glaze a little more.
Purple mouth, iron foot
紫口铁足. On Ge ware the glaze runs thin at the rim, letting the dark iron-rich clay show purple-brown, and the unglazed foot fires near black. The darker band round this tile's edge is that thin rim.

The tile is drawn at 12 cm square. Cell sizes are measured by filling each closed region between cracks.
