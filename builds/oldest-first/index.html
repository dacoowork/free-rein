    How it scores

    Every pair of cards you put the right way round is worth a point: ten pairs, ten points a round.

      Moving cards

      Drag a card by its grip, or use the up and down arrows on each card. With a keyboard, tab to an arrow and press it.

      The years

      Each year is the first time the thing was sold, patented, printed or switched on in the form we'd know. Where a story is murkier, the note on the card says so.

      Pairs, not places

      Getting one card badly wrong only costs the pairs it breaks. Swap two neighbours and you lose a single point.
