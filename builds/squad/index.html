Drawn from strokes

Each numeral is a skeleton of straight strokes, like the block figures cut from heat-transfer vinyl. Weight sets the stroke, corners set how strokes meet and end, and outlines are the same skeleton drawn wider underneath.

Reading distance

A number is printed about 30 cm tall. The model asks how far away its strokes stay wide enough to pick out on a moving player, then cuts that distance as the edge contrast with the shirt falls. Wide styles that have to shrink to fit the back lose height and reach.

Patterns

On stripes, hoops and halves the number sits on two colours at once, so the edge has to contrast with both. A white outline vanishes on the white stripe and a black one on the black; a red figure stands apart from both.
