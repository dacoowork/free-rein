Your practice (this browser only)

    Why a pad? Following a shape with your eyes is easy to drift from; pressing turns it into something your hand does too, so you notice when you've hurried. Nothing is scored. The line only shows you where you were.

    These are ordinary relaxation patterns, not treatment. If you feel light-headed, stop and breathe normally.
