Drawdown.

Write a weaving draft the way a handweaver does: thread each warp end onto a shaft, tie shafts to treadles, and choose a treadle for every pick. The drawdown shows where warp rises over weft, and the cloth beside it shows what comes off the loom.

A float is a stretch of yarn that passes over several threads without being bound in. Short floats make a firm cloth; floats of seven or more catch on fingers and buttons. Tromp as writ treadles the pattern in the same order it is threaded, which turns a point threading into diamonds.
