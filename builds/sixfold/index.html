In the window

Why twelve. A hexagon folded in half, then into thirds, then in half again makes a 30° wedge twelve sheets thick. Every cut lands six times around the centre, and each copy is mirrored across its fold. That is the same sixfold mirror symmetry an ice crystal grows with. Cuts that cross a fold edge open into holes; cuts on the open edge shape the outline.
