    How the map is drawn

    Every city is invented by rules. Lines run only at 0°, 45° and 90° and never turn back sharper than a right angle. No two lines share a stretch of track, every line meets another, and stations sit about two grid squares apart. Labels try eight positions each and take the first one that clears the lines and the other names.
