Tap a bar to light up that billion in the grid. Hatched bars are United Nations projections.

Growth is slowing: the UN expects the population to peak at about 10.3 billion in the 2080s, after which it begins to fall.

Milestone years are the commonly cited UN and US Census Bureau dates (1804, 1927, 1960, 1974, 1987, 1999, 2011, 2022). Years in between are interpolated, so treat single-year figures as estimates, good to a few tenths of a billion. The 2025 figure of 8.2 billion is approximate.
