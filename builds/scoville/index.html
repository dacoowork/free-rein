    Where it sits on the Scoville scale

    The shelf

    Heat

    Capsaicin spreads through the whole batch, so the sauce's Scoville rating is each chilli's rating weighted by its share of the total weight. Chilli figures are typical fresh-pod values; real pods vary by two or three times either way.

    Acid

    Food that stays above pH 4.6 can grow botulism in a sealed bottle. Peppers and fruit buffer acid, so it takes roughly a quarter of the batch as vinegar to bring a fresh pepper mash under pH 4. The bench models that buffering, but only roughly. Always check the finished sauce with a pH meter or strips before you bottle it.

    Fermenting

    A lacto-ferment needs 2 to 5% salt by weight of the mash. Over two to four weeks the bacteria turn sugars into lactic acid and bring the mash down to about pH 3.6. Vinegar and lime are added after, so they are left out of the salt sum.
