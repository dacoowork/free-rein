Lot Twelve

A two-player sealed-bid auction. Pass the device back and forth.

How to play

Eight lots go under the hammer. You each start with 100 coins to spend across all eight. Before every lot, each of you privately sees an appraiser's estimate: a 24-coin range that always contains the true value, but each of you gets a differently centred one. Talk, bluff, compare notes aloud, or say nothing. Then each of you secretly bids. Highest bid wins the lot and pays it; a tie withdraws the lot and nobody pays. At the end your score is the true value of your lots plus your unspent coins. Beat your rival to win.
