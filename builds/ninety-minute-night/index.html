Sleep cycle planner

Wake between the waves.

A night runs in cycles of roughly ninety minutes. Waking at the end of one, in light sleep, feels far easier than being pulled out of deep sleep halfway through.

Your night

REM, dreaming Light sleep N1–N2 Deep sleep N3
Cycles vary from about 80 to 110 minutes between people and through the night, so treat these as good targets rather than exact science. Most adults do best on five or six cycles.
