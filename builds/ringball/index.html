The Ring

Halcyon is a wheel, two hundred metres in radius, turning once every thirty-two seconds. People live on the inside of the rim, where the floor pushes up on them hard enough to pass for gravity. Nothing else about it is like gravity.

How it's played

  1. Three a side. Two teams of three share a court with five hoops, each hung on a post at a different place and height.
  2. One ball, one touch. Catch it, then throw it. No dribbling, no walking with the ball, and no putting it down.
  3. Through the hoop. A ball that passes through a lit hoop scores a point for the team that threw it. Only one hoop is lit at a time.
  4. Lag is yours. The ball doesn't know the floor is moving. Where it comes down is the thrower's problem, not the referee's.
  5. The floor is out of bounds. A ball that lands before it scores goes to the other side. Hubward walls are padded, because balls reach them.
  6. Five minutes a period, because after that the stand starts to feel it.

Words from the rim

Spinward
The way the floor is travelling. Right, on every court drawing.
Antispinward
Back the way you came, against the turning floor.
Hubward
Up, towards the axis. Hubward is also where the air gets thin of weight.
Lag
The sideways drift of a ball while it hangs in the air, and the reason for a lot of shouting.
Gym guess
A throw aimed as if the floor stood still. Every rookie's first, and last, cheap point.

Why the ball curves

Once a ball leaves your hand nothing is pushing it round with the ring any more, so it flies in a straight line through space, at the speed you and the floor had when you let go. The floor keeps turning underneath it. From the outside (the little dark window above) the ball never bends at all.

To someone standing on the rim that looks like a sideways force, so the ball seems to bend. A ball thrown straight up at 15 m/s hangs for and lands ahead of the thrower, spinward. Throw it twice as fast and it lands about four times as far ahead.

A smaller ring turns faster and curves everything harder. That is why Halcyon's court is on the widest stretch of the rim.

Ringball is invented. The physics isn't: every throw here is computed exactly in a ring of 200 m radius spinning for 0.8 g.
