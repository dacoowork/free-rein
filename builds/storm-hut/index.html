Storm Hut

A text adventure. You've reached an unstaffed mountain hut just as the storm closes in. Type what you do, and get through the night.

2,860 m · unstaffed
sleeps 6 · stove, no wood
WhereMain room Warmth

    How to play

    Type short commands: look, examine stove, take pot, go up, out, in, inventory. Tap any bold word to add it to the line.

    Stuck?

    hint nudges you toward the next thing, undo takes back your last move, and restart begins the night again.

    Tonight

    Six small things make a night in a hut bearable. You can sleep whenever you like; how you wake depends on how many you managed.
