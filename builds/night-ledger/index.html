Times are local to the chosen place. Sunrise and sunset use the sun's upper limb with standard refraction (−0.833°); twilights end at −6°, −12° and −18°. "Moon‑free dark" counts astronomical night with the moon below the horizon or less than 5% lit. Positions use low‑precision formulas good to about a minute or two at mid latitudes.
