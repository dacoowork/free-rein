The small print

Fitting. The Twin-Port ceiling unit draws condensate from the room's own air and returns it, so a weather never adds water to your home. It only rearranges it. Snow is real ice and melts within ninety minutes of switching off.

Not for use in conservatories, lifts, aircraft, or any room containing a piano, a tortoise or an unsealed manuscript.

Lightning in the Thundercloud is a cold-plasma discharge of under 0.2 joules. It will not hurt you. It will reset most clocks.

Rainbows appear where the physics puts them: with the sun behind you, at about 42° from the point directly opposite the sun. We cannot move a rainbow for you, but you can move the sun.

Combinations are not separately guaranteed. Fairweather & Daughters accepts no responsibility for weather that two products make between them, including but not limited to thundersnow.

Returns. Weathers may be returned within 28 days in their original box. Please let fog settle before packing.
