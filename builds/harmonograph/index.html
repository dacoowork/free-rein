Harmonograph

Two pendulums swing a pen; a third rocks the table under the card. Friction drains them slowly and the pen spirals inward until it rests.

Victorian parlours used these machines to show what a musical interval looks like. Tune the swings to a simple ratio and the figure closes on itself; detune them a hair and it slowly turns.
